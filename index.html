Real-time Sensing

Soil moisture, temperature, humidity and rain sensors capture microclimate data to make precise irrigation decisions.

Crop Intelligence

Adaptive watering patterns using crop stage models and local soil-water relationships to boost yield and reduce stress.

Rainwater Harvesting

Roof and field catchment systems store filtered rainwater to power irrigation during dry spells—lowering mains demand.

IoT & Remote Monitoring

Web and mobile dashboards with notifications, data-logging and manual override for farmer control and analytics.

Technologies & Components

  • Microcontroller: ESP32 / Arduino (configurable)
  • Sensors: Capacitive Soil Moisture, DHT11/22, Rain Sensor, Water Level
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi, LoRa (for remote farms), Optional GSM
  • Actuation: Relay / Motor Driver for pumps and valves
  • Power: Solar panel + battery backup; low-power sleep modes
  • Software: Lightweight REST API, Dashboard (React/Vue) and Mobile Alerts

Vision

AgroSmart aims to democratize precision irrigation for smallholders and commercial growers alike. By combining sensor-driven decisions, crop-specific intelligence, and sustainable water capture, the platform supports resilient farming, reduced operational cost, and smarter resource stewardship.

Impact

  • Conserve freshwater resources and reduce utility costs
  • Improve yield consistency and crop quality
  • Enable data-driven farm management and forecasting

Deployment & Scalability

AgroSmart is modular: start with a single zone and scale to multi-zone farms with distributed LoRa nodes and a central aggregator. Offline logging ensures safety during connectivity outages.
